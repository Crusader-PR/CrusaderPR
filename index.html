How The Services Work Together

Crusader runs outreach as one connected system: digital foundation, message, reach and measurement. Results from each cycle inform the next plan.

  1. 01

    Foundation

    Domains, website, email and analytics configured, with every link tagged.

    Every click traced back to the send that drove it.

  2. 02

    Message

    Video, design and copy produced for each audience and channel.

    Picture texts and flyers built for each push.

  3. 03

    Reach

    Texts, email, digital ads, streaming TV and mail, scheduled around key dates.

    The full audience reached four times in six days.

  4. 04

    Results

    One live tracker records every send, opt-out and reply.

    Every reply became a warm lead for follow-up.

↻ Results inform the next plan, and the cycle repeats.
