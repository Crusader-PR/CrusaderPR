Approach

Crusader Public Relations starts with text messaging to reach voters quickly and mobilize support, then adds targeted digital advertising, OTT commercials produced in its in-house studio, website design and social media.

Its graphic design team produces campaign materials, and print and direct mail delivers them to voters. Each engagement includes strategic planning matched to the client's goals.
